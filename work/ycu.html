YCU

I am the sole engineer on YCU, a subscription learning platform shipping to iOS, Android, and web. The stack is Expo/React Native and Next.js 16 on a Turborepo/pnpm monorepo, backed by Supabase. I own the product from Postgres schema and billing through App Store review.

Billing is dual-path: Stripe on the web, Apple In-App Purchase on iOS, with trials, entitlement checks, and partner code redemption. User-generated content — group chat, video and challenge comments — goes through an LLM safety classifier and an admin moderation queue that surfaces surrounding context for review.

I drove the app through Apple App Review across 50+ versioned releases. It is live on the App Store. The site is ycu.app.
